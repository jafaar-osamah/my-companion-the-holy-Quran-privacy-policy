Privacy Policy for My Companion: The Holy Quran

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. Overview

My Companion: The Holy Quran ("the App") is developed and maintained by Jaafar Osamah ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping your Quran reading experience private and secure.

2. Information We Collect

  • No Personal Account Data: The App does not require registration, login, or submission of personal information such as your name, phone number, or location.
  • Local Preferences & Offline Cache: Your chosen translation, reciter, theme (Light/Dark), bookmarks/reading progress, and downloaded Quran texts for offline use are stored locally on your device using browser/WebView storage (localStorage and IndexedDB). This data never leaves your device.
  • Network Requests for Content: When you are online and load or sync Quranic text, translations, or audio recitations, the App connects to public Quran content APIs (such as Quran.com APIs) solely to retrieve the requested verses and audio files. Standard technical connection data (such as IP address) is processed temporarily by those content servers to deliver the data.

3. Customer Support

If you voluntarily contact us via Email (jaafarosamah23@gmail.com) or WhatsApp from the Settings screen, we receive the message and basic device/translation details you choose to send solely to respond to your support request.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App does not sell your personal data, and it does not include third-party advertising networks or behavioral tracking SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Deletion

Because all app settings and offline synced Quran chapters are stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App.
